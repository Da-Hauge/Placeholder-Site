MCU Tracker

Data & methodology

What every field in the tracker means, where the numbers came from, and what has and hasn't been individually re-verified. Captured 2026-09-08.

Scope

The default (core) list covers every Marvel Studios production — films, Disney+ series, Marvel Studios Special Presentations and official one-shots — from Iron Man (2008) up to and including Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars, both of which already have official release dates even though unreleased. Titles announced for Phase Seven without a confirmed date (an untitled X-Men film, Ghost Rider, Black Panther III, Armor Wars, Nova, a second Shang-Chi) are deliberately left out for now — an undated title would need re-checking on every visit anyway, and the tracker doesn't yet have a way to flag "rumoured" separately from "confirmed."

Turning on "Include non-MCU Marvel films" adds a second, clearly-flagged list: Sony's non-MCU Spider-Man-related films (the Raimi and Garfield Spider-Man films, Venom, Morbius, Madame Web, Kraven), 20th Century Fox's X-Men / Fantastic Four / Deadpool films made before the Disney-Fox merger, the original Blade trilogy (1998–2004), and the pre-2019 Marvel Television shows (Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Agent Carter, Inhumans, Runaways, Cloak & Dagger, and the Netflix Defenders shows). None of these are treated as canon by Marvel Studios itself, which is why they are off by default and carry no importance tier.

What "importance tier" means

Essential titles carry a plot or character thread that later, essential entries depend on. Recommended titles add meaningful context but aren't strictly required. Optional titles are self-contained side stories. Skippable titles are the ones most published viewing-order guides agree add little to the throughline — that does not mean they're bad films, several are well reviewed.

This is inherently a judgement call, synthesised from public consensus rather than handed down by Marvel Studios. It will not match every fan's own ranking, and it is not updated automatically — it reflects the guides available on 2026-09-08.

What isn't covered yet

  • Undated Phase Seven announcements (see "Scope" above).
  • Individual episode titles/synopses — episodes are tracked by season and number only.
  • Audience scores for most series (Rotten Tomatoes doesn't publish an aggregate audience score for every season).
  • Posters and live rating refresh — only available if you add your own TMDb API key in Settings; see the tracker's Settings dialog and privacy policy for what that does.

Found a mistake?

This dataset is hand-maintained, not pulled from a live database, so it can go stale or simply be wrong in places. Reports are welcome — see Impressum for contact details, or open an issue against the source repository.
